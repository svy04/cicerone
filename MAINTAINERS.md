# Maintainers

| Role | Who | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Maintainer | [@svy04](https://github.com/svy04) | Everything |

One person maintains this fork. `.github/CODEOWNERS` routes review on the paths a
quiet mistake would be expensive on, and every one of them routes here.

Questions belong in [Discussions](https://github.com/svy04/cicerone/discussions).
Security reports belong in this repository's **Security** tab — see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The upstream project [santifer/career-ops](https://github.com/santifer/career-ops)
has its own maintainers; they do not maintain this fork. Past contributions that
this fork inherited are credited in [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md).
